01 / Systems

Distributed systems are the center of gravity.

At Red Hat, I contribute upstream across the PyTorch ecosystem, with distributed training and communication as the primary focus. Edge runtimes and developer tooling extend that work across the full path to deployment.

Distributed training & communication

I work on collective operations, transport layers, process-group lifecycle, and multi-backend coordination. The goal is bounded, understandable failure behavior and correct execution across hardware topologies.

Framework internals

I trace correctness problems through autograd, tensor abstractions, checkpointing, parallel training, and compiler-adjacent execution paths—turning subtle integration failures into durable upstream fixes.

On-device & edge inference

I extend portable operator behavior, repair kernel correctness, and improve selective-build plumbing so models can move from Python development workflows to resource-constrained targets with fewer surprises.

Diagnostics & integration

I build tracing and adapter infrastructure that exposes framework call paths for debugging and profiling. Clear errors, useful tests, and reliable integration are part of the product surface.
